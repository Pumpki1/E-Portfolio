Dog Emotion Recognition

A deep learning study that classifies the emotional state of dogs from images into four categories, which are happy, sad, angry, and relaxed, in support of automated animal health monitoring. The project compares a custom convolutional neural network against a MobileNetV2 transfer learning model, both trained on the same balanced dataset of 800 labeled images prepared through resizing, normalization, and data augmentation. The transfer learning model reached 80% accuracy on unseen test data, compared with 26.1% for the custom network, which shows the advantage of pre-trained features on small datasets.

AI Based Air Monitoring System

An ESP32 based indoor air quality monitor that detects hazardous gases and responds to them automatically. Four MQ series gas sensors are read every 100 milliseconds and cleaned through median, moving average, and Kalman filtering, and an Edge Impulse anomaly detection model running on the device scores how far the combined readings deviate from normal air. The system identifies the dominant gas, grades the air as Normal, Low, Moderate, or Dangerous, and drives LED indicators, a buzzer, an LCD, and an exhaust fan that starts at the dangerous level. A web dashboard shows live readings, thirty minute aggregated trends, and a sensor correlation matrix, raises on screen alerts, and stores its data in Supabase. The project began as an Arduino Uno prototype that learned its normal air baseline on the board.

Lead Developer, Embedded Systems Developer, IoT Developer
Software and Tools

CPU Escape Box

An interactive mini arcade system that teaches computer architecture through gameplay. Players trace register level instructions across Easy, Medium, and Hard levels within a five minute countdown, using a joystick and push buttons on a 2.8 inch display. The system includes user registration and profile management with create, read, update, and delete functions, a Top 20 leaderboard, and persistent storage in the Arduino EEPROM. Completing every level in time energizes a solenoid lock that opens a reward compartment, and the electronics are housed in a cyber themed arcade cabinet.

Laboratory Access through Face Detection

A face recognition access control system for laboratory rooms, designed to replace manual logbooks and physical keys with automated identification. A Raspberry Pi enhances each webcam frame through a digital signal processing chain of bilateral filtering, adaptive histogram equalization, and sharpening, detects faces with a Haar cascade, and matches them against registered users using deep learning face embeddings. A recognized person receives an Access Granted message on an LCD and a buzzer confirmation while a servo motor opens the door, and unrecognized faces are denied with a short retry delay. Every access attempt is recorded in a Supabase database.

Lead Developer, Embedded Systems Developer, Computer Vision Developer

Automated RFID System for Laboratory Borrowing and Record Logging

An automated system that replaces the paper based process for borrowing laboratory equipment. A student or faculty member taps an RFID card on an RC522 reader connected to an ESP32, which checks the card against the registered users, records the borrow or return transaction, and displays a confirmation. Unregistered cards are denied, and repeated taps in quick succession are logged as a single transaction. Transactions are stored in a Supabase database.

ATAKI Micromouse

An autonomous micromouse robot built on a two wheel chassis and controlled by an Arduino Uno, designed to navigate a maze without human input. Three ultrasonic sensors measure the distance to the walls ahead and on both sides, and a motor driver controls the two drive motors. The navigation logic is reactive: the robot stops at a front wall, checks the open directions on its left and right, and steers using the side sensors to stay clear of the walls.

Lead Developer, Robotics Engineer, Embedded Systems Developer

AudiVo

A website, with the tagline Where Text Speaks and Voice Writes, that converts text to speech and speech to text across languages. Users type English text and hear it spoken in Korean, Mandarin, Japanese, or Tagalog, or they speak in one of those languages and receive the translated English text.
