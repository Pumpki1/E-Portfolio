Profile

Who I Am

Khen Mhar B. Ilao is a fourth year Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering student at Jose Rizal University. He builds practical solutions where software meets hardware, working across artificial intelligence, embedded systems, IoT, and full stack development.

His path into Computer Engineering began with a genuine liking for computers and a fascination with how a computer and an application actually work, especially games. That curiosity never faded. Learning new things is what drives him most, and he is at his best in the moment he discovers something he did not know was possible and decides he must learn it.

He is seeking internship and entry level opportunities where he can apply his technical skills, gain industry experience, and contribute to innovative engineering projects while growing as a Computer Engineer.
