01Who I Am

Khen Mhar B. Ilao is a fourth year Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering student at Jose Rizal University. His focus is the point where software meets hardware, designing and building practical systems across embedded systems and IoT, full stack development, and artificial intelligence and machine learning.

His technical foundation spans Python, C++, Java, and JavaScript, platforms such as Arduino, ESP32, and Raspberry Pi, and tools including TensorFlow, OpenCV, Flask, Flutter, and Supabase. He has applied this knowledge in projects that include a dog emotion recognition model, an AI based air monitoring system, a face detection laboratory access system, and the PawsomeCare mobile application.

What sets him apart is curiosity and a habit of turning what he learns into working systems, through both coursework and self initiated projects. He is adaptable, collaborative, and comfortable leading a team, and he approaches problems analytically with a commitment to building reliable and ethical technology. Outside his studies, music, films, video games, and reading keep him creative and balanced.
